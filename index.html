Studio + Reactions

She Only Reacts To
What She Actually Saw

The old “give a model a bag of frames and ask for eight beats” pipeline was deleted in August 2026. It was fluent and it was making things up. The Reactive Engine replaced it with five separated stages, and no model is called until the pixels say something happened.

  • ✓ Trigger: cheap OpenCV motion, HUD-region deltas and audio onsets flag the candidate moments — GPU still optional
  • ✓ Perceive: three chronological frames (before / during / after) go to a vision model as one strip, which returns strictly-typed JSON
  • ✓ Decide: plain, boring Python decides whether that earns a line, spread across the whole clip
  • ✓ Write & guard: she writes it in her own voice, and a guard bins it if she invented a weapon, a killer, or a number the HUD never showed
  • ✓ Sensitivity presets — Chatty, Balanced, Picky — plus per-game HUD profiles, cooldowns and a minimum-confidence floor
  • ✓ Every gated moment keeps its reason, so a quiet video can be diagnosed instead of relitigated at 2 AM
  • ✓ Solo or duo, plus live gameplay commentary, sharing the same motion controller as the live show
