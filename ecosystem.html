The UwU Ecosystem

A collection of experiments and products exploring what happens when AI stops being a chat window and becomes a persistent character that can remember, speak, act, work, create and exist across digital worlds.

One identity. Many bodies. The long-term direction is a shared character core with specialized surfaces for streaming, Discord, VR, games, desktop, work and developer tools.
